What is CREON?

It's a forum for Itenas students to innovate and excel in the field of robotics. Actively involved in research and development of technology through its divisions: KRAI, KRTI, and KRBAI. With a competent team and a high spirit of innovation, CREON is committed to becoming a leading center for academic robotics and contributing to the advancement of national technology.
