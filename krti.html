Who We Are

What is AEROTENS?

AEROTENS (Aeronautica Team of Engineering Itenas) is a multidisciplinary robotics team from Institut Teknologi Nasional (ITENAS) Bandung. Representing CREON in the KRTI 2026 VTOL division, our team brings together students from Informatics, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Product Design, and Visual Communication Design to develop an autonomous drone capable of completing emergency service missions.

Mission Phases

Mission 1: Manual Navigation

Navigate manually through Gate 1 and Gate 2 to reach WP1. Transition from manual to autonomous mode at this checkpoint.

Mission 2: Autonomous Double Gate + Med Kit Drop

Autonomously traverse the 1-meter Double Gate corridor from WP1 to WP2. Identify the red target zone and release the First Aid Kit.

Mission 3: Triple Gate Navigation

Navigate through the 2-meter Triple Gate corridor using collision avoidance sensors and ArUco marker detection for position correction at WP4.

Mission 4: Black Line Tracking

Track the dashed black line on the field surface using the downward-facing camera, navigating from WP4 to WP5.

Mission 5: Final Gate & Autonomous Landing

Pass through the final Single Gate and execute a precision autonomous landing on the designated landing pad.

Autonomous Navigation

Jetson Nano processes visual data in real-time for GPS-denied autonomous flight using ArUco markers and computer vision.

Visual Detection

Dual ArduCam camera system (horizontal and vertical) for obstacle detection, line tracking, and target identification.

Med Kit Delivery

Custom-designed gripper mechanism capable of carrying and autonomously releasing the First Aid Kit at the designated drop zone.

Collision Avoidance

Ultrasonic sensors and emergency landing system ensure safe flight operations with automatic landing on signal loss.

Our Progress

What We're Working On

A snapshot of the key activities and milestones our team has been focusing on as we prepare for KRTI 2026.

Autonomous System Development

Configuring the Jetson Nano microcontroller, calibrating ArduCam cameras, and establishing the wiring infrastructure between the microcomputer and Pixhawk flight controller for autonomous flight operations.

Mechanical Customization

Designing and fabricating the custom gripper mechanism for secure med kit handling, engineering the propeller guard for safety compliance, and customizing the landing gear for stable autonomous touchdown.

Field Construction

Building a regulation-compliant practice course including 2 single gates, 1 double gate, and 3 triple gates that match the official KRTI 2026 competition field specifications.

Creative & Branding

Fulfilling mandatory KRTI social media engagement regulations through research progress updates, team branding content, and building public awareness of the AEROTENS team identity.
